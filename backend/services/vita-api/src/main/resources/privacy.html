Vita

A quiet log of meals, water & movement.

Draft (v0). This page describes how Vita handles data today. Wording is pending final review; the practices below are what the software actually does.

What Vita collects

Only what the app needs to show you your own log: your email address (to sign you in), an optional display name, and the entries you create — meals, water, workouts, weight, notes, and the plans you import. Vita has no goals, no scores and no streaks, and it does not build an advertising profile. There is no third-party analytics or ad tracking in the app.

How it is stored

Personal content is encrypted before it is written to the database, with AES-256-GCM under a key that belongs to your account alone. When you delete your account that key is destroyed first — every stored value encrypted with it becomes permanently unreadable — and the rows are then deleted. Deletion runs after a 7-day grace period, and signing in during that window cancels it.

Health Connect and health data

On Android, Vita can read activity data from Health Connect (active energy, steps, workout sessions) if you turn it on. That data stays on your device: it is stored in the app's local database and is never uploaded to Vita's servers. You can revoke the permission at any time in Health Connect.

Photos and documents

Photos of meals and imported plan documents are uploaded so they can be read once and turned into entries. The uploaded file expires automatically after 30 days and is removed from storage.

Interpretation

Text, voice transcripts, photos and plan documents you submit are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be turned into structured entries, and are not used to train models. Voice is transcribed on your device; the audio itself never leaves the phone.

Your choices

You can export your data or delete your account from the account screen in the app. Deleting the account removes everything described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to the address listed on Vita's store listing.
